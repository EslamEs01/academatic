- Eight `teacher-*` portal pages are Teacher self-service; `teachers`, `teacher`, and `teacher-performance` are administrator surfaces.
- `teacher-profile` and `teacher` are never interchangeable.
- `teacher-performance` is absent from Teacher portal navigation and copy.
- User-visible Teacher pay/salary/payroll/payment/compensation/wage/bonus/commission/financial-ranking concepts are zero in both locales.
- Scores, ranks, leaderboards, percentiles, ratings, invented charts, and calculated utilization/performance engines are zero.
- Teacher rosters contain minimum learning identity; guardian/private student contact and locality are absent. A Teacher may view their own allowed contact on self-profile.
- `teacherAbsent` and `studentAbsent` remain distinct.
- Every terminal action is classified and truthful. No fake saved/submitted/assigned/approved/created/updated/deleted/sent/uploaded/completed/authorized state exists.
- No sensitive value is added to storage, URLs, generated fixtures, attributes, logs, or screenshots.
- Protected Spec-043 meaning may not be weakened.
