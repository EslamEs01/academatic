## Scope-creep red flags (reject on sight)
- A new standalone page/base for invoices/payments/monthly-invoices/salaries/staffSalaries/banks (see
  `page-count-contract.md`).
- A computed total, outstanding balance, net/profit/loss, revenue, VAT/tax, or any payroll/salary AMOUNT figure
  anywhere in `finance.html`.
- A `<canvas>`/chart element or a charting dependency.
- Unlocking `classSalaryReport` or adding a `finance-analysis` nav item (both must stay as declared in
  `nav-completion-contract.md`).
- A `type=file` or `type=password` input, a raw credential/API-key/webhook secret, or a `href="#"`.
- Any final action (Create/Generate/PDF/Send/Mark-Paid/Confirm/Refund/Add-bank/Import/Reconcile/Approve/Export)
  that fakes success instead of gating on `backendRequired`.
- Any invoice/payment/salary/bank row that actually mutates status on click.
- Editing `enhance.js`/`build-html.mjs`/`i18n.js`/`package.json` for any reason.
- Touching any nav item beyond the 6 declared in `nav-completion-contract.md`.
- Widening `fixtures/finance.js` beyond a pure additive grouping helper (no new amount data, no new computed field).

## Stop conditions (halt + report)
- Public HTML count ≠ 115 after build.
- A standalone page is required for any of the 6 unlocked surfaces without a declared, build-verified amendment
  first.
- Any surface requires a computed total/balance/profit/loss/revenue/tax or a salary/payout AMOUNT figure.
- Any final action fakes success; any invoice/payment/salary/bank row actually mutates.
- A payment gateway, accounting engine, or backend/API/network dependency is introduced; `type=file`/`type=password`
  appears.
- `package.json`/`enhance.js`/`build-html.mjs`/`i18n.js` need to change.
- A role-law / no-fake-money / finance-Spec-009 / admin-menu-50 / teacher-pay-free protected assert would need
  weakening beyond the one sanctioned `nav010` rescope.
- `finance.html` cannot land its 3 new tabs without disturbing an unrelated page's `#page-body` or the shared
  sidebar beyond the 6 status-flip anchors.

## Acceptance
- `git diff --stat` touches only files listed under "Allowed to create/modify" above (plus the regenerated
  `public/*.html` build output).
- `fixtures/finance.js` diff is empty, or — if unavoidable — is a single additive, figure-free grouping helper with
  no changed row data.
- `package.json`, `build-html.mjs`, `enhance.js`, `i18n.js` all show **0 diff**.
