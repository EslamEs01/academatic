## Scope-creep red flags (reject on sight)
- A new standalone page/base for any of the 5 surfaces (see `page-count-contract.md`).
- A computed score, rank, GPA, percentage, or rubric total anywhere in reports/families/students.
- A `<canvas>`/chart element or a charting dependency.
- A money/salary/payout figure appearing in `reports.html` (must stay finance-free forever) or anywhere in families/students.
- A `type=file` or `type=password` input, a raw credential/secret, or a `href="#"`.
- Any final action (generate/export/PDF/send/save/publish/reclassify) that fakes success instead of gating on `backendRequired`.
- Any row/status/roster mutation (family reclassify, student result/evaluation edit) that actually persists.
- Editing `student.js`/`family.js`/`result-summary.js`/`evaluation-rubric.js` when a fold on `students.js`/`families.js` alone would suffice.
- Touching any nav item beyond the 5 declared in `nav-completion-contract.md`.
- Widening the diff into `enhance.js`, `build-html.mjs`, `i18n.js`, or `package.json`.

## Stop conditions (halt + report)
- Public HTML count ≠ 115 after build.
- A standalone page is required for any of the 5 surfaces without a declared, build-verified amendment first.
- Any surface requires a computed metric/score/rank/GPA/percentage/rubric-total or a chart/`<canvas>`.
- Any money/salary/payout figure is required in reports/families/students.
- Any final action fakes success; any row/status/roster actually mutates.
- Backend/API/network/dependency is introduced; `type=file`/`type=password` appears.
- `package.json`/`enhance.js`/`build-html.mjs`/`i18n.js` need to change.
- A role-law / no-fake / finance-Spec-009 / admin-menu-50 / reports-finance-free protected assert would need weakening.
- `families.html`/`students.html`/`reports.html` cannot land their new tabs without disturbing an unrelated page's `#page-body`.
